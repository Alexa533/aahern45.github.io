Interests & Personal Projects

A collection of my passions and creative pursuits

Photography

In my teens, I was gifted with a Canon Rebel EOS T7 after it was noted by many that I would take photos on my cell every chance I got to capture something uniquie and beautiful. I use the same camera to this day to take shots of the night sky and its contents, landscapes, macro shots of nature, portaits of others, and much more. I took multiple photography classes and have recently took a trip to a nearby mountain throughout the late night to before sunrise to capture a meteor shower with friends. Holding a moment forever in a photograph feels like the best way to look back on the meaningful moments of life.

Piano

I first started learning the piano when I was in 7th grade after meeting the best piano instructor that I would work under all the way into my senior year of high school. Playing the piano brought out a hidden skill in me and love for music that I did not even know that I had, until I learned that I can accomlish whatever I put my mind to when it comes to this instrument. When I was younger, I also practiced the guitar and hope to pick it up in the near future.

Reading

When I am not taking time for my studies or hobbies, I enjoy reading books and novels ranging from the classics to modern stories. Throughout high school, I found myself consumed by the works we would read in English classes, and felt a true calling to the way that an author's writing can have many different emotions provoked, different hidden messages/meanings, and how they can capture the very essence of life and its moments.

Writing

As someone who loves to read, I also love to create my own stories and works. I have been drawn to writing for as long as I can remember. I find myself thinking of great lines to add to a future book, poem, or letter and writing them down no matter where or when. I mostly write on my laptop, but I also enjoy taking to pen and paper, to feel more connected with my creativity and the composition of more authentic-feeling pieces.

Art

In various different meduims, I create works that I feel inspired to put my own spin on. I particularly enjoy using Prismacolor wax pencils and alcohol markers, as well as pastels. This is a work that I entered into my high school's art show. This was my second time using prismacolors, and I felt extremely accomplished by the way that I was able to pull out the color and depth of this wolf's eyes with only alcohol markers and colored wax pencils over the ink!
